Common questions

How do I back up my CTRAC data?

Your CTRAC data is included in your normal iOS device backups. Make sure iCloud Backup (or iTunes/Finder backups) is enabled in iOS Settings. CTRAC itself does not transmit your data to any server.

How do I delete all of my CTRAC data?

Delete the CTRAC app from your device. This removes all locally stored holdings, transactions, goals, budgets, watchlist items, notes, and receipt images created by the app.

Are prices real-time or delayed?

CTRAC's prices are simulated from a deterministic seed so your historical view stays consistent. The app does not request network access to obtain market data.

Does CTRAC connect to my broker?

No. CTRAC does not connect to any broker, exchange, or trading venue. It is a self-contained journaling and tracking tool.

Can I import or export my data?

Use the export option inside the app's Settings to produce a local backup file you control. CTRAC does not send this file anywhere.

Is CTRAC a financial advisor?

No. CTRAC is an educational tool. It does not provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.
