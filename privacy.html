Summary

CTRAC is a private, on-device investment journal. It does not require an account, does not connect to any broker or exchange, and does not transmit your financial data to any server operated by us or by third parties. The app is built so that what you record stays on your iPhone, under your control.

In short: No account. No analytics. No tracking. No ads. No network calls for your data.

Data We Collect

We collect nothing about you. CTRAC does not maintain a server, does not run a database of users, and does not receive telemetry from the app. Information you enter — such as holdings, transactions, goals, budgets, watchlist items, notes, and receipt images — is stored locally on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, address).
  • Account credentials of any kind.
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or fingerprinting data.
  • Location data.
  • Usage analytics, click events, or crash reports that include personal data.
  • Financial data transmitted to our infrastructure.

How Prices Work

CTRAC does not connect to any market data feed, broker API, or exchange endpoint. All prices displayed in the app are generated locally from a deterministic seed so that your historical view remains stable across sessions. As a result, the app does not need network access to render prices, charts, or insights, and never sends your holdings or activity over the network for the purpose of obtaining quotes.

Receipts & Photos

If you choose to attach a receipt or note image to a transaction, that image is stored on your device as part of your local journal data. CTRAC does not upload these images to any server and does not share them with third parties. Removing a transaction in the app removes the associated image from your device's local storage.

Device Backups

Your data is included in your normal iOS device backups if you have iCloud Backup or iTunes/Finder backups enabled. Backups are governed by Apple's privacy and security practices and by your own iOS settings. CTRAC itself does not transmit your data off-device.

You can remove CTRAC's data at any time by deleting the app from your device.

Permissions

CTRAC is designed to require the minimum set of iOS permissions necessary for its functionality. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, or Face ID. Camera and photo library access is requested only when you choose to attach an image to a journal entry, and that access is optional.

Children's Privacy

CTRAC is intended for adults managing their own finances. The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because we collect no information at all, this policy applies uniformly regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy in a material way, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, surface a notice inside the app. Continued use of CTRAC after such changes indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
