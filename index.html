Privacy is the architecture.

CTRAC does not connect to any broker, exchange or trading venue. There are no accounts and no servers. Your data stays on your device.

No account, no login

You install, you use it. We do not know who you are, and there is nothing to sign in to.

No tracking, no analytics

No third-party SDKs. No advertising identifiers. No crash reporting that includes your data.

Simulated prices

All prices are generated from a deterministic seed so your historical view stays stable — and the app never reaches the network for quotes.

Full details are available in our Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about what CTRAC does — and does not — do.

Does CTRAC connect to my broker or exchange?

No. CTRAC does not connect to any broker, exchange or trading venue. It is a self-contained journaling and tracking tool.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your iPhone. Backups follow your normal iCloud device backup settings, controlled by you in iOS.

Does CTRAC provide financial advice?

No. CTRAC is educational. The app includes an in-app risk acknowledgement reminding you that nothing here constitutes financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Are prices real-time?

No. Prices are simulated from a deterministic seed so historical views remain consistent for you across sessions. The app does not request network access for quotes.
